Welcome to Wild Rescues. Our mission is to rescue comma rehabilitate comma and release wildlife back into their natural environment. We are a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping injured wildlife.

Our staff provides around-the-clock care for wildlife in need. We also provide long-term care for wildlife that cannot be released back into their natural environment.
